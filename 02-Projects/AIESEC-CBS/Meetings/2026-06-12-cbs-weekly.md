## Topics Discussed

### LCP Application & Election Timeline
- Application booklet details the "Leadership Triangle" (Lead, Manage, Represent) and measures of success (retention rate, Net Promoter Score).
- Election: Saturday June 22nd, 13:00.
- 24-hour "silent period" from June 21st 13:00 - candidates cannot discuss the election with LC members.

### Membership Criteria & Legal Status
- CBS currently has 0 votes in national legislation (hasn't met 100% of criteria, incl. exchange realizations).
- Only LCPs of "Full Member" LCs can vote on national motions.
- Reviewed Compendium Sections 2.2 and 2.3 on member rights and the General Meeting's power to overrule via a two-thirds majority.

### New LC Structure (the "Tigers" model)
- Move to a core Leadership Team (President, Vice President, Head of Finance) to reduce individual stress.
- Products (GV, GT) led by a "Head of Product" rather than a VP; Team Leaders focus on Marketing or Customer Service.
- Lennert: "I would really love to try this because... it just works better... for a student organization."

### Membership Models & Engagement
- Tiered membership idea: Active (full benefits/work), Passive (supporters, lower fee), Event-based (pay per attendance).
- Idea to establish a "Board of Advisors" of seniors and alumni for quarterly guidance.
