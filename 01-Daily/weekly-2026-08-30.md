### 2. Sleep anchor: No screens after 22:00 for 3 nights

The Aug 28 insomnia and Aug 29 YouTube binge share a root cause: screens at night. Pick 3 nights (suggestion: Mon-Wed) and enforce a hard phone/laptop cutoff at 22:00. Use the briefing checkout section to report adherence (yes/no). Track next-morning energy subjectively (1-5 scale) in the briefing. This creates a tiny feedback loop without needing a new app.

### 3. Briefing-to-action bridge: Add a `closed_loops` field

Append one line to each morning briefing's YAML front matter:

```yaml
closed_loops: "TALENT pricing fix -- replied to Bill"
```

This takes 10 seconds, uses the existing commit infrastructure, and gives the weekly synth a signal of what actually happened. If the loop is not closed, write `closed_loops: "none"`. After 7 days we will have a completion rate metric for the first time.
